# gandr-theory-stone-duality

The finite case of Stone duality for gandr: free distributive lattices in normal form, finite Birkhoff duality between posets and distributive lattices of sets, and monotone maps against lattice homomorphisms.

## Synopsis

**What.** A space can be given by its points or by the algebra of its observations. For a finite space the two descriptions are a finite poset and the distributive lattice of its down-sets. The crate goes from each to the other. A `FinitePoset` gives its down-sets as a `SetRing`. A `SetRing` gives its `Spectrum`: the members that are no union of smaller members, ordered by inclusion. A `MonotoneMap` between posets gives the homomorphism between their lattices by preimage, and `MonotoneMap::of_preimages` reads the map back. The free distributive lattice on at most 64 generators has its own normal form: a `Polynomial` is an antichain of `Term`s, and a `Homomorphism` substitutes polynomials for generators. Its spectrum is the cube, and `CubeMap` is a monotone map of cubes.

**Why.** The face formulas of finite shapes generate lattices of subshapes, and the presented-spaces frame reads a space and its algebra of observations as one object. The spectrum of a lattice of subshapes is the least face at each point. The monotone polynomials over two-point carriers are the free distributive lattice, and the maps of cubes are its homomorphisms. The shapes crate's suites read both facts through this crate.

**How.** Every set is one machine word: a term over at most 64 generators, a set of at most 64 elements or points, a truth table on a cube of at most six axes. Normal forms are canonical, so equal elements compare equal. The least member at a point is one intersection per member, so the spectrum costs the carrier times the members. An enumeration that can grow past its input, the down-sets of a poset or the closure of a family of sets, takes a `Budget` and refuses the input past it.

## Provided features

- `Term`, `Polynomial`: the free distributive lattice in normal form, with meet, join, order and evaluation at a vertex.
- `Homomorphism`: substitution of polynomials for generators, composition and the identity.
- `Vertex`, `Table`, `CubeMap`: the cube as the spectrum of the free lattice, truth tables of polynomials, and monotone maps of cubes read as homomorphisms and back.
- `ElementSet`, `FinitePoset`: a finite poset closed from a relation, its principal down-sets, down-closure, the cube as a poset, and the lattice of its down-sets.
- `SetRing`, `Spectrum`: a finite distributive lattice of sets generated by a family, its least member at each point, its join-irreducible members as a poset, and its representation by the down-sets of that poset.
- `MonotoneMap`: a monotone map of finite posets, its preimage homomorphism, and the map read back from a homomorphism.
- `DualityError`: every refusal, by the index it concerns.

## Examples

```rust
use gandr_theory_stone_duality::{Budget, ElementCount, ElementSet, SetRing};

// The faces `{port, tls}` and `{host, port}` of a three-point carrier.
let faces = [ElementSet::from(0b110), ElementSet::from(0b011)];
let lattice = SetRing::generated(ElementCount::from(3), &faces, Budget::from(64)).unwrap();
let spectrum = lattice.spectrum().unwrap();
// The least faces: `{port}`, `{host, port}` and `{port, tls}`.
assert_eq!(spectrum.points(), &[ElementSet::from(0b010), ElementSet::from(0b011), ElementSet::from(0b110)]);
```

## Where the detail lives

The free lattice, its normal form, truth tables, homomorphisms and maps of cubes are in `src/free.rs`. Finite posets, lattices of sets, spectra and monotone maps are in `src/finite.rs`. The indices and verdicts are in `src/index.rs`, and the refusals in `src/error.rs`.
